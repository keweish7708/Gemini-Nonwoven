    Double Board Needle Punching Machine

    Single needle loom including two needling sections

    Double board needle punching machine is crucial nonwoven equipment, distinguished by its two needle boards (or needling zones), which provide cost-effectiveness and process optimization advantages. By combining different stroke types, the double board needleloom can meet various production process requirements.

    Needling frequency: ≤600 rpm, ≤1200 rpm
    Working width: 2000mm~9000mm
    Request Information
    Applications

    Double board needle punching machine is utilized in the primary production processes of various nonwoven products, including but not limited to carpets, felts, linings, geotextiles, filtration media, oilcloth base fabrics, shoe midsoles, synthetic leather substrates, and more.

    Specifications
    Needling frequency ≤600 rpm ≤1200 rpm
    Working width 2000mm~9000mm
    Stroke range 20~60mm
    Stroke type Double Up-stroke Double Down-stroke Tandem Up & Down
    Needling zone (▼▼) ; (▲▲) ; (▼▲)
    Needle density ≤7500n/m x2 Needle Board
    Features
    • Automatic variable frequency control.
    • Advanced segmented modular design offers high structural strength, ensuring stable and reliable operation.
    • Computer-controlled random needle distribution results in superior appearance and quality of products.
    • Double board needleloom features dual crank shaft structure and dual tandem drive.
